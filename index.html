Acquisition costs & purchase GST

Acquisition costs & purchase GST

Provisional assumption: purchase price is exclusive of 10% GST. Vacant possession does not confirm the contract's GST treatment. The six allocations below are editable budget placeholders, not quotes or calculated duty. They preserve the prior $45,000 allowance; GST on the property is additional.

Ancillary allowances are net permanent costs; credits on those fees are not separately modelled. Transfer duty is a manual allowance: confirm the dutiable value including any GST, regional concession eligibility, and the property's commercial and industrial property tax status. Land tax / future CIPT must fit the operating allowance or be added in a later revision.

Capex reserves & management time

Capex reserve & workload assumptions

The base case still funds $4,000 of capex each year, increasing 3%. The default assumes it is all spent. A lower spend percentage retains the unspent amount as a separate cash reserve included in net wealth. Cash after reserve funding is unchanged. The renting alternative remains $300/week plus GST.

Management time is an optional economic cost, not a cash payment or tax deduction. Zero hours means workload is not valued in the base case. The separate adjusted result compounds that annual time value at the equity-return assumption.

Component schedule — central base case

Still unconfirmed: GST-credit eligibility, transfer duty / CIPT status, cost quotes, building condition and leasing demand. Income tax, capital gains tax, depreciation benefits and small-business CGT concessions are not calculated. No tax saving is assumed. These are deterministic scenarios, not a quantified risk-adjusted return.
